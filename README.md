# Silicon & Substrates — Process, Reticle Limits, and Multi-Die Packaging

A grounded tour of the foundry physics behind every NVIDIA GPU — TSMC's process lineage from 16FF through 4N/4NP to N3 and A16, the 830 mm² reticle limit and how Blackwell broke through it, NV-HBI die-to-die, CoWoS-S vs CoWoS-L vs CoWoS-R packaging, defect density and yield-driven SKU binning, the CoWoS supply bottleneck, and where the industry is heading with backside power. Includes an interactive process and packaging picker.

**Live site:** https://brendanjameslynskey.github.io/NVIDIA_GPU_25_Process_and_Packaging/

Part of the [NVIDIA GPU Architectures series](https://github.com/BrendanJamesLynskey/LLMs#nvidia-gpu-architectures).
